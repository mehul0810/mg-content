1 = Beginner / low strategic value  
2 = Generic / low authority  
3 = Moderate but upgradable

5 = Core authority asset

- Discusses scale, architecture, risk, reliability

Anything scoring ≤2 becomes a merge, deindex, or prune candidate.

# STEP 3: CLASSIFY EACH URL

Assign one of:

### 1. Authority Asset
- Enterprise-aligned
- Position 5–20 or improving
- Supports audit funnel
- Strengthens cluster

### 2. Upgrade Candidate
- Ranking but generic tone
- High impressions but low CTR
- Misaligned intro
- Needs enterprise reframing

### 3. Merge Candidate
- Overlapping topic
- Keyword cannibalization
- Thin content
- Weak backlinks

### 4. Prune (Delete + Redirect)
- Zero impressions in 12 months
- No backlinks
- Off-positioning
- Beginner tutorial
- Dilutes authority

### 5. Deindex
- Useful but not strategic
- Low ranking potential
- Internal operational reference

### 6. Neutral Keep
- Stable traffic
- No strategic harm
- No strong upside
