# mehulgohil.com — Blog Categories Structure

This document defines the official blog category structure for mehulgohil.com.  
All future articles must be assigned intentionally based on positioning and authority alignment.

The site is positioned as:
Enterprise WordPress Engineer  
WPVIP Advanced Professional WordPress Developer Certified  
Focused on Architecture, Performance, Reliability, and Risk.

---

## 1. Enterprise WordPress

**URL:**  
https://mehulgohil.com/category/enterprise-wordpress/

**Description:**  
Enterprise WordPress engineering for high-scale, revenue-critical platforms. Covers architectural decisions, performance engineering, system reliability, data modeling, risk mitigation, and operational discipline required when WordPress supports core business operations. Focused on scalability under load, upgrade safety, infrastructure design, and preventing structural fragility before it impacts revenue.

**Use This Category When:**  
- The article discusses WordPress at scale  
- It involves risk, reliability, or system fragility  
- It addresses enterprise or high-traffic environments  
- It strengthens audit positioning

# Category Assignment Rules

1. Every article must have one primary category.
2. Enterprise WordPress may be added as a secondary category for authority-building articles.
3. Avoid creating new categories unless structurally necessary.
4. Categories must support enterprise positioning — not traffic chasing.
5. No beginner-focused categories.
